What I Work On

I am an Associate Professor in Engineering at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) and serve as the Principal Investigator of the Offshore & Marine Digital Learning Lab. My work focuses on bridging research and industry to develop intelligent, data-driven solutions for the maritime and offshore sectors.

My research interests span digital transformation, data analytics, adaptive neural systems, control engineering, electrification, cyber-physical systems, autonomy, smart sensing, real-time decision support, and operability prediction. I am passionate about applying these technologies to create practical solutions that improve efficiency, safety, and sustainability.

Beyond research, I contribute to the wider engineering community as an Associate Editor for the IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering and a Steering Committee Member of the Asian Control Association. I previously served as Chair of the ASEAN Sub-Committee on Marine Science and Technology (2024–2025).

Before joining academia, I held technology leadership roles in the offshore and marine industry, including Technology Lead (Digitization) at Keppel FELS, Programme Manager (Control) at the Keppel Offshore & Marine Technology Center, and Scientist at the Technology Centre for Offshore and Marine Singapore (TCOMS). These experiences continue to shape my approach to translating cutting-edge research into real-world engineering solutions.
