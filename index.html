HELM

The Heterogeneous Embodied Learning Machines (HELM) Group is where robotics, AI, and the ocean collide in a good way. We're fascinated by physical AI: machines that don't just crunch data on a screen but actually go out and do things in the real world. Our roots are in marine and offshore engineering - autonomous boats, underwater vehicles, smart control systems, and greener, more efficeint ships. But our robot family has been growing. These days you'll also find a humanoid finding its feet, a four-legged robot patrolling the lab and aerial drones buzzing. What ties them all together is one big question we love chasing: how do you teach a machine to understand the world well enough to act in it reliably, safely, and on its own? We mix hands-on field testing with serious science, and we have a lot of fun doing it.
